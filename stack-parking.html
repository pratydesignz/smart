Common questions

How much extra parking capacity can we expect?

Most two-level stack installations roughly double capacity in the same footprint; puzzle systems with more levels can go higher depending on headroom and structural load.

Do you check if our basement can take the load?

Yes — a structural check is part of every survey, along with a layout drawing showing exactly how many bays and levels fit before you place an order.

Is it difficult for staff to operate?

No — day-to-day operation is push-button, and we train your staff or valet team as part of handover.
