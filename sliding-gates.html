Common questions

How heavy a gate can you automate?

Our standard range covers leaves up to 4000 kg. For anything heavier we specify a higher-torque industrial operator after checking the track and civil base during survey.

Can you automate the sliding gate we already have?

In most cases yes. We check the frame, wheels, rail alignment and foundation during the site survey and quote for any civil or fabrication work needed before fitting the operator.

What happens during a power cut?

A manual release key disengages the motor so the gate can be pushed by hand. Battery or UPS backup can also be specified so the gate keeps operating through short outages.
