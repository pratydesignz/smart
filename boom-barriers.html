Common questions

Can the barrier work with our existing RFID or ANPR system?

Yes — loop detectors, RFID readers and ANPR cameras are commonly integrated into the barrier controller, and we can also wire into an existing ticketing setup.

How many vehicle movements a day can it handle?

Our high-cycle barrier models are rated for continuous use at secured gates and parking entries seeing hundreds of movements a day — we size the model to your traffic during survey.

Is there a backup if the power goes out?

A manual release lets the boom be raised by hand, and UPS-backed operation can be specified so the barrier keeps working through short outages.
