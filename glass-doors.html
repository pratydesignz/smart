Common questions

Can the door integrate with our access control system?

Yes — we regularly wire glass door operators into existing card reader, biometric or fob-based access control, along with fire alarm interlocks where required.

Is it safe for high-footfall entrances?

Motion and infrared safety sensors prevent the door closing on someone mid-way through, and the toughened glass and aluminium track are rated for continuous commercial use.

What if the power fails?

The door has a fail-safe manual break-out, so it can be pushed open by hand even without power.
