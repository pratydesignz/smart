Common questions

Underground or above-ground arm — which is better?

Underground operators keep the gate posts clean and are common on higher-end residences; above-ground arms are simpler to service and cost less. We recommend based on your gate weight and how the pillars are built.

Will it still work if the power goes out?

Yes — every swing gate operator we fit ships with a manual release, and battery backup can be added so the gate keeps cycling through outages.

How long does swing gate installation take?

A straightforward two-leaf swing gate is usually commissioned in a single day once the site survey and drawing are approved.
